About this tool

This is an independent, volunteer-built, nonpartisan lookup for Pennsylvania’s November 3, 2026 general election. It exists to answer one question: who is on my ballot, and where do they stand?

Where the information comes from

  • Who’s on your ballot: the Pennsylvania Department of State’s official candidate listing. Every certified candidate appears, listed identically.
  • Your districts: the U.S. Census Bureau’s public geocoder. Your address is sent only there, and is never stored or logged by this site.
  • Issue positions: each candidate’s own campaign materials, voting records, and public statements — every entry carries a link to its source and the date.

How position summaries are written

  • Summaries paraphrase what the candidate has said or done, in neutral wording, with no evaluation or characterization.
  • Nothing is ever inferred from a candidate’s party. If a position can’t be verified from the candidate’s own record, the table says “No public position on file” — that means unverified, not that the candidate has no view.
  • Every race is compared on the same fixed set of seven issues, and every candidate in a race gets the same research effort.
